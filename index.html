A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Research at the interface of chemistry and biology

I hold a PhD in Chemistry from the University of Delhi. My research focuses on understanding how small molecules interact with proteins and how those interactions influence protein structure, dynamics, and biological function.

I combine computational approaches-including molecular docking, molecular dynamics simulations, and binding-energy calculations-with experimental techniques such as fluorescence spectroscopy, circular dichroism, and biochemical assays. I am interested in using these complementary methods to develop and test hypotheses relevant to drug discovery.

SELECTED RESEARCH

Research projects

Integrating computational chemistry and experimental methods to investigate molecular recognition, protein interactions, and bioactive compounds.

01

PhD research · Protein interactions

Noscapine-based ionic liquids and serum albumin

Investigating the interaction of a noscapine-based ionic liquid with human serum albumin through fluorescence spectroscopy, UV–visible spectroscopy, circular dichroism, molecular docking, and molecular dynamics simulations. The aim is to connect binding-site interactions with changes in protein structure and dynamics.

Fluorescence spectroscopy Circular dichroism Molecular docking GROMACS
02

Computational chemistry

Protein-ligand simulation and binding-energy analysis

Analysing molecular dynamics trajectories to investigate protein stability, conformational changes, ligand interactions, and residue-level contributions. Computational analysis combines structural metrics with MM/PBSA or MM/GBSA calculations to support mechanistic interpretation.

GROMACS Trajectory analysis MM/PBSA MM/GBSA
03

Materials and bioactive molecules

Functional materials and biologically relevant molecules

Research experience spanning ionic-liquid chemistry, biomolecular interactions, nanocomposite hydrogels, and functional materials. These studies connect chemical synthesis or material design with characterization and evaluation of application-relevant properties.
